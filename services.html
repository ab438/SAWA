How we help

Services designed around you

The details matter. Here is what you can expect when you shop with us.

01

Delivery to your door

Place your order with your correct phone number and address. Our team will contact you to confirm the details and arrange delivery. Delivery time and fees can be confirmed when your order is placed.

02

Cash on delivery

Pay for your order when it arrives. Our current checkout supports cash on delivery, so you can confirm your order without entering card details online.

03

Returns & exchanges

Need a different size or have an issue with your order? Contact our team before sending an item back so we can guide you through the available exchange or return steps.

04

Order confirmation

After submitting your order, WhatsApp opens with your order details ready to send. Please press Send so our team receives your request.

05

Personal assistance

Have a question about a product, size or order? Message us and our team will help you with the information you need.

06

Careful selection

We aim to present clear product details and images to help you choose the piece that suits your style.
